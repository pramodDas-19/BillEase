# BillEase Workspace Rules & Guidelines

## 1. Product Positioning & Target Market
- **BillEase targets ALL types of businesses**: Service providers, agencies, freelancers, consultants, contractors, traders, physical goods sellers, retail shops, and SMBs.
- Features, terminology, and workflows must be versatile and inclusive for any business model. Do not restrict assumptions to any single niche.

## 2. Architecture & Code Integrity
- Preserve existing billing, invoice, quotation, and payment functionality.
- Prioritize high visual aesthetics (tactile neo-clay UI, smooth animations, responsive design).
- Maintain multi-tenant data isolation and strict GST compliance (CGST/SGST, IGST, HSN/SAC, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B).
