# BillEase Target Market & Business Scope

## Core Rule: BillEase Targets All Types of Businesses
- **Universal Scope**: BillEase is built and positioned to target **ALL types of businesses** — including service providers, agencies, freelancers, consultants, contractors, traders, retail shops, and small-to-medium enterprises (SMBs).
- **Inclusive Product Design**:
  - Do NOT restrict features, terminology, or assumptions exclusively to freelancers or creative agencies.
  - The Product & Service Catalog, Invoices, Quotations, and Payments must remain versatile, supporting both **services** (hours, projects, consulting) and **products/goods** (pieces, units, quantities, physical goods, HSN/SAC codes).
  - When designing future features, pricing plans, onboarding flows, and UI elements, always ensure they are accessible, useful, and relevant across diverse business verticals.
